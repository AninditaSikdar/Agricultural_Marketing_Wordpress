🏗️ State Logistics Framework

4-Tier Farm-to-Consumer Supply Chain Architecture

End-to-end multi-tier logistics handling produce movement from rural farm gates to consumer hubs and export pack houses.

Tier 1
🚜

Farm ➔ Primary Collection Hub

  • ✓ Village PACS / FPO Aggregation Point
  • ✓ Farm-gate sorting, weighing & pre-cooling
  • ✓ Small transport (Tata Ace, 3-wheelers)
Tier 2
🚛

Collection Hub ➔ APMC Mandi / Godown

  • ✓ Inter-mandi bulk movement & storage
  • ✓ WDRA Warehousing & cold chain intake
  • ✓ Medium commercial fleet (407 & 14ft Eicher)
Tier 3
🏭

Mandi / Yard ➔ Food Processor Hub

  • ✓ Bulk supply to rice mills & dal mills
  • ✓ Commercial contracts & quality assaying
  • ✓ Heavy multi-axle freight (6W & 10W trucks)
Tier 4
🛒

Processor ➔ Retailer & Export Ports

  • ✓ Sufal Bangla mobile vans & retail kiosks
  • ✓ Reefer container transit to Kolkata Port
  • ✓ Consumer branded retail delivery
