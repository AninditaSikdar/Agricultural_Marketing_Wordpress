Mandi & Auctions How are daily Mandi rates collected, verified, and updated on this portal?
▼

Mandi prices are synchronized in real-time through the unified AGMARKNET 2.0 and e-NAM gateway connected to APMC checkposts across all 23 districts of West Bengal.

  • Morning Session: Market Secretaries and Enumerators record wholesale arrivals and initial opening rates between 06:00 AM and 09:30 AM.
  • Modal Price Verification: The most representative (modal) wholesale trade value is calculated after major auctions conclude by 11:30 AM.
  • Public Broadcasting: Validated figures are reflected immediately on this portal, via SMS alerts, and displayed at digital Krishak Bazar price boards.

Mandi & Auctions How can a farmer participate in live transparent e-auctions at Krishak Bazars?
▼

Participating in transparent electronic auctions at any of West Bengal's 186+ Krishak Bazars is completely free for growers:

  1. Advance Gate Entry: Drive your vehicle to the APMC Gate. Present your Krishak Bandhu ID or Voter Card to receive an electronic Lot Identification Number (Lot-ID).
  2. Quality Assaying: The on-site Agmark laboratory conducts a quick 10-minute assessment of moisture, foreign matter, and grain size.
  3. Live Bidding: Your lot details are projected onto the bidding display. Registered traders bid electronically, ensuring you receive the highest market-driven price.
  4. Instant Settlement: Sale agreement and weighbridge weight receipts are generated digitally, with payment credited via DBT within 24 hours.

Mandi & Auctions What legal remedies exist if a registered trader or buyer delays payment after purchase?
▼

Under Section 17 of the West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, buyers and commission agents are legally mandated to disburse full payment to farmers on the same day or within 24 hours of sale completion.

If payment is withheld or delayed without justification:

  • Lodge an on-the-spot formal complaint with the APMC Market Secretary at the market yard office.
  • Register an online Grievance Docket on this portal or call the Kisan Helpline at 1800-180-1551.
  • The APMC has statutory powers to suspend the trader's trading license, invoke their bank security deposit, and disburse the outstanding principal plus penal interest directly to the farmer.

Schemes & Grants How do I apply for the "Amar Fasal Amar Gari" transport subsidy scheme?
▼

The "Amar Fasal Amar Gari" scheme provides financial assistance to small and marginal farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and Self Help Groups (SHGs) to acquire customized agri-transport vehicles (mini trucks, motorized three-wheelers, or solar e-carts).

  • Subsidy Quantum: 50% capital subsidy on the on-road vehicle cost, up to a ceiling of ₹1,25,000 per individual beneficiary.
  • Eligibility: Permanent resident of West Bengal holding a Krishak Bandhu card, owning cultivable farmland, and possessing a valid commercial driver's license.
  • Application Process: Submit your application online via the Schemes & Subsidies section or physically at your District/Sub-Divisional Agricultural Marketing Office along with land records, bank passbook, and vehicle quotation.

Schemes & Grants Is Krishak Bandhu registration mandatory to receive marketing incentives?
▼

While routine retail sale of produce at Krishak Bazars and village haats is open to all farmers, having a registered Krishak Bandhu ID (KB-ID) is strongly recommended and mandatory for capital grants:

  • Zero-Friction DBT: Subsidy disbursements (such as vehicle purchases or pack-house grants) are credited directly to your bank account via Aadhaar-linked DBT.
  • Assuring Priority: KB-ID holders receive priority booking for government cold storage chambers, subsidized freight movement, and subsidized seed/fertilizer allocations.

Sufal Bangla & FPOs How can Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) become official suppliers to Sufal Bangla?
▼

Sufal Bangla, managed by the Paschimbanga Agri Marketing Corporation Ltd. (PAMCL), procures fresh vegetables, fruits, pulses, and organic honey directly from farmers at remunerative rates:

  1. Empanelment Application: Submit FPO registration certificate, board resolution, GSTIN (if applicable), and list of member farmers to sufalbangla@wb.gov.in.
  2. Aggregation Hub Linkage: PAMCL maps your FPO to the nearest regional aggregation hub (e.g., Singur, Dhaniakhali, Krishnanagar, or Malda).
  3. Daily Indent & Weighment: Sufal Bangla issues digital procurement indents daily based on urban demand. Weighment is conducted transparently at hub weighbridges.
  4. Direct Bank Settlement: Payment is cleared within 48 to 72 hours via electronic transfer directly to the FPO's official bank account.

Cold Storage & WDRA How do farmers obtain low-interest bank loans using electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWR)?
▼

An e-NWR (electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipt) enables farmers to pledge their stored crop as collateral with commercial banks, avoiding distress sales during harvesting gluts:

  1. Deposition: Deposit your produce (e.g. potatoes, paddy, pulses) at any WDRA-accredited cold storage or state warehouse.
  2. Digital Assay & Token: The warehouse manager inspects quality standards and generates a tamper-proof digital e-NWR in the central repository (CCR/NERL).
  3. Instant Loan Sanction: Approach any partner commercial or cooperative bank. The bank pledges the digital receipt online and disburses up to 75% of current market valuation directly into your account at concessional interest rates.
  4. Repayment & Release: Repay the loan when market prices rise and electronically release the stored commodities for sale.

e-Bijak & Licensing What is an e-Bijak invoice and why is it legally required for wholesale trade?
▼

e-Bijak is the official digital invoice and transit ledger system for all regulated agricultural commodities traded within West Bengal APMC market yards:

  • Statutory Breakdown: Accurately computes taxable commodity value, statutory APMC market fee (0.5% - 1.0%), authorized commission charges, and certified weighbridge fees.
  • Prevents Middleman Exploitation: Eliminates unauthorized deductions, ensuring farmers receive transparent accounting slips with zero hidden cuts.
  • Checkpost Transit Clearance: Embedded QR codes enable enforcement inspectors and highway flying squads to verify consignments instantaneously via mobile scanners.

e-Bijak & Licensing What is the application process for obtaining a new APMC Trader or Commission Agent License?
▼

Operating as a commercial buyer, wholesaler, or commission agent within regulated market yards requires a statutory license issued by the concerned Regulated Market Committee (RMC):

  1. Online Submission: Register on the departmental portal with PAN card, Aadhaar card, proof of business premises, and trade license from local body.
  2. Statutory Security Deposit: Remit the prescribed security deposit and annual fee through the GRIPS e-challan system.
  3. Physical Yard Verification: The Market Secretary conducts verification of premises and weighment equipment within 7 working days.
  4. Digital License Issuance: Approved certificates with unique License Registration Numbers (LRN) are issued online with 3-year validity.
