Recommended Cold Preservation Protocols

Official state standards for optimum storage life and quality maintenance.

🥔

Potato (Table & Seed)

Storage temperature: 2°C - 4°C with 85%-90% relative humidity. Regular CIPC treatment for sprout suppression.

🧅

Onion & Garlic

Storage temperature: 0°C - 2°C with 65%-70% relative humidity. Good forced air ventilation is essential to avoid fungal neck rot.

🥭

Fruits & Vegetables

Pre-cooling at 10°C followed by preservation in controlled atmosphere chambers at 4°C - 8°C for maximum crispness.
