About Agricultural Marketing Department

Fostering competitive and transparent market ecosystems for agricultural produce across West Bengal.

Agricultural Marketing Department started functioning independently following Notification No. 6420/MW&C/AM/O/9M-29/2011 dated 31.10.2011. Prior to this notification, it functioned as a vital wing of the Agriculture Department under the MW&C Branch. Today, it spearheads transformative digital and physical market infrastructure across the state.

The department operates under the purview of The West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, 1972 and The West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Rules, 1982. The landmark amendment in 2014 further accelerated direct farm-to-buyer transactions, modernized terminal markets, developed rural market link roads, and attracted private-public partnerships to safeguard farmers against distress selling.

Under State Budget Demand No. 4, the Department actively executes major capital infrastructure projects through the West Bengal State Agricultural Marketing Board (WBSAMB), Directorate of Agricultural Marketing, Paschimbanga Agri Marketing Corporation Ltd. (PAMCL), and NSTIAM.

🏪 Modern Rural Markets

Krishak Bazar Network

Purpose-built agricultural marketplace complexes constructed by WBSAMB in every block to eliminate middlemen and enable direct farmer selling.

Dedicated Covered Stalls & Open Sheds

Weatherproof retail stalls, wholesale transaction bays, and spacious auction platforms designed specifically for local farmers and self-help groups.

❄️

Integrated Cold Rooms & Storage

On-site temperature-controlled cold rooms and packhouses ensuring zero distress sale of perishable vegetables and seasonal fruits.

⚖️

Electronic Weighbridges & APMC Billing

Certified electronic weighbridges and computerized check-counters guaranteeing 100% accurate net weight and fair market price calculation.

🏨

Farmer Rest Sheds & Citizen Amenities

Kisan Niwas overnight accommodation, clean drinking water points, banking & ATM kiosks, and sanitary complexes for farmers arriving from distant villages.

🚚

Direct Link Roads & Fleet Loading Bays

Reinforced asphalt approach roads with dedicated loading and unloading docks accommodating transport trucks and Sufal Bangla mobile vans.

📊

Digital e-NAM Integration

Equipped with high-speed digital terminals to broadcast real-time AGMARKNET prices and conduct transparent e-auctions across nationwide buyers.

🌾 MSP Guarantee & Food Security

Paddy Procurement Operations

Kharif & Rabi season centralized procurement infrastructure safeguarding farmers from distress selling with guaranteed MSP and 72-hour direct DBT bank credit.

🌾

Centralized Procurement Centers (CPCs)

Strategically located procurement centers at Krishak Bazars and regulated market yards equipped with moisture meters, sieves, and tarpaulins for fair grading.

💳

Direct DBT Bank Settlement

Eliminates intermediaries with 100% direct electronic transfer of MSP payments into farmer bank accounts linked with Aadhaar and Krishak Bandhu IDs.

📱

Biometric Farmer Verification

Advanced biometric POS machines and SMS token scheduling ensure that genuine small and marginal farmers receive timely selling slots without queuing.

🚛

Grain Logistics & Buffer Stocking

Coordinated dispatch to WBSAMB silos and State Food & Supplies godowns supporting Khadya Sathi public distribution and emergency grain reserves.

🏛️ Institutional Ecosystem

Affiliated Bodies & Directorates

The statutory, operational, and development institutions working under the Agricultural Marketing Department.

Regulatory & Administrative Wing

Directorate of Agricultural Marketing

Enforces the APMC Act 1972/2014, issues trader licenses, monitors daily arrivals and price indices across mandis, and supervises Regulated Market Committees.

Statutory Infrastructure Board

West Bengal State Agricultural Marketing Board

Apex statutory board responsible for planning, financing, and constructing agricultural market yards, Krishak Bazars, specialized link roads, and cold chain facilities.

Commercial & Retail Enterprise

Paschimbanga Agri Marketing Corp. Ltd.

The state government corporation operating Sufal Bangla retail kiosks, mobile vending vans, commercial procurement, and consumer price stabilization operations.

Research & Training Institute

NSTIAM (Enabling Employability)

Apex residential training center delivering vocational certifications, quality assaying labs, post-harvest research, and FPO leadership enablement.
