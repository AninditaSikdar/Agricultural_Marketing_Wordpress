How to Apply for Agricultural Subsidies

Simplified 4-step digital onboarding process for individual growers and FPOs.

Step 1

Online Application

Fill out the simplified grant application form with your Krishak Bandhu ID / Aadhaar card details.

Step 2

Document Verification

District Agri-Marketing Officer (DAMO) inspects land records and quotations submitted.

Step 3

Administrative Sanction

Receive formal in-principle sanction letter with direct subsidy allocation code.

Step 4

Direct Bank Transfer (DBT)

Subsidy amount is credited directly to the beneficiary's linked bank account upon asset delivery.
